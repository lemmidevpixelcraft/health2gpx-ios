Privacy Policy

Health2GPX · Effective: September 2026

In short: Health2GPX does not operate an advertising or analytics profile and does not require an account. Apple Health workout data is processed on your device. Network access is used only for features you request, such as weather data, optional iCloud sync and Apple services.

Apple Health data

Health2GPX accesses workout, route and related health data only after you grant permission through Apple Health. This data is used to display, analyse and export your workouts.

Apple Health data is processed locally on your device and is not uploaded to the developer's servers. Exported files leave the app only when you explicitly save or share them.

Weather data

Health2GPX uses a privately operated Open-Meteo server for route weather, forecasts and historical workout weather. For historical route-wind analysis, Apple Weather (WeatherKit) is preferred when available, with Open-Meteo used as a fallback.

To provide these features, approximate route coordinates, searched locations and workout dates/times may be sent to the relevant weather provider. No HealthKit health values such as heart rate or calories are required for these weather requests.

Open-Meteo data is provided under the CC BY 4.0 licence. Apple Weather attribution and source information is available inside the app where Apple Weather data is used.

Optional iCloud synchronization

If you enable iCloud synchronization, imported workouts and favourites can be stored in your private iCloud database so they are available on your Apple devices. Apple Health data remains in HealthKit and is not copied into the app's iCloud synchronization database.

iCloud processing is provided by Apple and is subject to your Apple Account and Apple's privacy terms.

Purchases and App Store services

Subscriptions and in-app purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Health2GPX receives only the purchase status needed to unlock features; the developer does not receive your payment-card details.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Health2GPX does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use third-party analytics or cross-app tracking.

Notifications

If you enable notifications, Health2GPX may create local notifications on your device, for example for workout-related information. Notification preferences are stored locally.

Data retention

The developer does not maintain a user account database for Health2GPX. Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it or delete the app. Data stored through optional iCloud synchronization is controlled through your Apple/iCloud account.

Your choices

You can revoke Apple Health, location and notification permissions at any time in iOS Settings. You can also disable iCloud synchronization in Health2GPX settings.
