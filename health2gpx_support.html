Health2GPX Support

Health2GPX turns Apple Health workouts into useful route files and training insights, including GPX export, maps, weather and route analysis.

Need help?
Email lemmidevpixelcraft@gmail.com. When reporting a bug, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, app version and a short description of what happened. Do not send sensitive health data unless it is necessary for the support request.

Getting started

  • Allow Health2GPX to read the Apple Health workout data you want to use.
  • Select a workout in the app.
  • Review route, statistics, weather and analysis where available.
  • Export or share the workout in the desired format.

If workouts are missing

  • Open iPhone Settings and verify Health permissions for Health2GPX.
  • Make sure the workout exists in Apple Health.
  • Some route features require GPS/location data recorded with the workout.

Weather and wind analysis

Weather features require an internet connection. Health2GPX uses Open-Meteo for route weather and historical weather. Historical route wind can additionally use Apple Weather (WeatherKit) when available, with Open-Meteo as fallback. Older workouts or some locations may have limited historical data.

iCloud sync

Optional iCloud synchronization covers imported workouts and favourites. If synchronization is unavailable, verify that you are signed in to iCloud and that iCloud access is enabled for the app.

Purchases

If Pro features are not recognized after a purchase, use the restore-purchases option in the app and make sure the device is signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase.

Privacy

Apple Health data is processed on your device. Network requests are used only where needed for features such as weather, optional iCloud synchronization and Apple services.
